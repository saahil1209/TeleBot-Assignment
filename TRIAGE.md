# Triage: which notes are worth developing

Applied to every note in `notes/` with `status: raw`. The output is a status change,
never a silent deletion - a note that does not clear the bar stays on disk as
`status: parked` with a one-line reason, because the reason is often that it is
early rather than weak.

## What the evidence says works

Her four published LinkedIn posts drew 47,000 impressions. The strongest single
piece was a seven-paragraph breakdown of why niacinamide concentration matters
more than the percentage printed on the label: 340 profile visits in 48 hours,
three wholesale enquiries. Her audience is 28-40, urban, and explicitly tired of
being sold to.

That is one data point, not a law. But it points the same way her voice guide
does: a mechanism that corrects an assumption, explained at length, ending in
something the reader can act on.

## Develop a note if it has all four

1. **A mechanism.** Something that happens for a reason she can explain - pH
   against a stability threshold, a base that determines whether an active
   arrives, a protocol inherited without being questioned. A note that only
   reports an outcome has no post in it yet.
2. **An assumption it knocks down.** The reader should finish holding a
   different belief than they started with. "Concentration on the label is not
   the thing that matters" is the model.
3. **Evidence she actually owns, or evidence she can name honestly.** Her own
   CoAs, returns, stability tests, repeat purchase rates. Failing that, external
   research cited generically. A note whose central claim cannot be sourced
   either way is parked, not written around.
4. **An exit.** A question the reader can put to a brand, a manufacturer, or
   customer service. If the note dead-ends in an opinion, it is not a post.

## Park a note if any of these hold

- It needs medical authority. She is a formulator, not a dermatologist.
- It names a competitor, or lands blame on a person rather than a system.
- The only claim in it is ahead of the data she has.
- It is a product announcement with no mechanism attached.
- It is a reaction with no fact under it - real feeling, nothing to brief.

## Parked is not dead

Two parked notes on the same mechanism often merge into one developable note.
Check `status: parked` before every drafting run.

## The arithmetic problem

She wants three posts a week. Sixty notes over eight months is 1.75 notes a
week, and the four criteria above will park a meaningful share of those. The
target and the intake do not currently meet.

Three honest options, in the order I would try them:

1. Draft more than one post per strong note. A mechanism with three consequences
   is three posts, not one. This is the only option that does not lower quality
   or ask more of her.
2. Raise capture rate. The habit already exists; the ask is volume, not change.
3. Lower the target to two a week. Four posts in eight months produced 47,000
   impressions. Two a week is a very large increase on her actual baseline.

Do not close the gap by loosening the four criteria. That produces the writer
she already fired.
